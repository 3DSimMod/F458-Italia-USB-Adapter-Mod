## About the Mod:
This adapter enables the Ferrari 458 Italia Wheel to be used as a standalone USB device on PC. 
<br>
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/F458 Italia Wheel.png" width="350">

### IMPORTANT - Torque limitation:
When using the F458 Italia Wheel, do not exceed 10 Nm of wheelbase torque.

Exceeding this limit may cause damage to the wheel structure or mounting interface, both of which are made of plastic.
<br>

### Installation guide:
###### 1. Unscrew all marked screws.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/1.png" width="400">

###### 2. Place the wheel facing upward.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/2.jpg" width="400">

###### 3. Open the wheel and unplug the cables marked in red.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/3.png" width="400">

###### 4. Remove the wheel rim.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/4.jpg" width="400">

###### 5. Unscrew the three screws securing the quick release.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/5.jpg" width="400">

###### 6. Remove the quick release.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/6.jpg" width="400">

###### 7. Unscrew the quick release cable.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/7.jpg" width="400">

###### 8. Connect the cable to the USB Adapter Mod, then secure it with the screw.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/8.jpg" width="400">

###### 9. Attach your 6x70 mm quick release to the USB Adapter Mod, then mount the assembly onto the wheel.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/9.jpg" width="400">

###### 10. Reconnect all internal cables.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/10.jpg" width="400">

###### 11. Close the wheel and screw it back together.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/11.jpg" width="400">

###### 12. Installation complete.
<img src="F458 Italia Guide Images/12.jpg" width="400">
